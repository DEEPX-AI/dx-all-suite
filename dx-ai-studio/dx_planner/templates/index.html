EdgeGuide methodology

How are recommendations calculated?

1. Data

Uses YOLO26 single-stream and multi-stream DX Benchmark measurements only.

2. Channels

T_sat = lowest measured total FPS
M     = largest measured stream count
        with per_channel_fps ≥ target
E     = floor(T_sat / target)

channels = M   if a measured count above M misses the target
         = max(M, min(E, 2 × max tested))   if every count holds it
         = E   if no measured count holds it

Measured when a tested stream count brackets your target; otherwise estimated from the measured total FPS, which stays nearly flat around saturation. Estimates stop at twice the highest tested stream count. A throttled measurement is kept — its FPS already ran at throttled clocks — and flagged.

3. Meets requirement

meets            = channels ≥ cameras
                   && latency_ms ≤ maxLatencyMs (if set)
meets (estimate) = the same, on an estimate
                   or with latency not measured

Latency is the single-stream benchmark latency. With a budget set and no latency measured, a result never counts as a plain pass.

4. Sorting

sort: meets → meets (estimate) → not throttled
      → priority → channels → pipeline FPS

Best performance ranks by measured camera-pipeline FPS (host + NPU end to end), not the NPU-only figure. Lowest power ranks by NPU TDP, then FPS per NPU watt — host power is not included. Contact DEEPX for pricing.

Notice — guide limits

  • Channel counts are measured where a tested stream count brackets your target, and otherwise estimated from the measured total FPS — each card says which.
  • Benchmarks were measured around 30 FPS per channel. At 15 or 60 FPS most figures are estimates — confirm on hardware for production.
  • Benchmarks cover the YOLO26 DX Benchmark matrix only — not your camera codec, pre/post processing, or multi-model pipelines.
  • EdgeGuide figures are not a 100% accurate spec sheet. For production sizing, quotes, and final channel counts, contact DEEPX.
